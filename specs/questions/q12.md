# Q12: Transfers of a lot

**Fixture status:** Examples are provisional until verified against the selected frozen snapshot, pinned BOR release, or versioned retrieval cache, as applicable. Existing local checks retain their stated query dates; they are not a frozen acceptance baseline. See [expected-answer policy](../roadmap.md#expected-answer-policy).

## Question

> What deed transfers are recorded for this lot in the selected period, and for the lots currently attributed to its Owner Portfolio? Which of them can be attributed to the currently identified owner?

## Who asks and why

It answers "does my landlord sell frequently, or hold?"

## Required content of an answer

1. A timeline of deeds for the lot: document id, document date, recorded date, amount, grantor and grantee.
2. The count of **deeds**, with the number that were not sales.
3. For portfolio lots: the same, with each transfer marked attributable or not to the currently identified owner.
4. The portfolio run, if portfolio lots are included.

## Caveats the answer must carry

- Count deeds, not lots. A multi-lot deed is one deed covering several lots.
- A `$0` deed is not a sale.
- Attributing a transfer to the current owner is derived. Current membership is not historical ownership.

## Worked examples

| Case | Input | What the answer shows | Why it matters |

| Sale and a no-money deed (anchor A) | 5413 7 Avenue | Two deeds since 2015: recorded 2021-02-26, $5,000,000 sale; recorded 2025-04-02, $0, the same name as grantor and grantee | The answer counts 2 deeds, one of them not a sale |

## Scenarios

- A sale recorded within a month of a new registration: both reported with dates, and the answer says nothing about intent.
- A lot sold as part of a multi-lot deed: reported once as a deed with the lots it covers.
