# Q7: Shared evidence between two entities or buildings

**Fixture status:** Examples are provisional until verified against the selected frozen snapshot, pinned BOR release, or versioned retrieval cache, as applicable. Existing local checks retain their stated query dates; they are not a frozen acceptance baseline. See [expected-answer policy](../roadmap.md#expected-answer-policy).

## Question

> What shared evidence (people, business addresses, resolved identities) do these two entities or buildings have, and which of it was set aside as non-evidence?

## Who asks and why

It is the question behind "is it the same landlord?"

## Required content of an answer

1. Each shared item with its type (person in a role, business address, DOS process address, document, resolved identity) and its source record.
2. Each item marked **used** or **set aside**, with the reason for setting aside.
3. How many other parties share each address, so a hub address is visible.
4. Whether either layer grouped the two, with the run.

## Caveats the answer must carry

- A name match is a lead, shown with its evidence.
- A masked or mail-drop address is set aside, and the answer says so.

## Worked examples

| Case | Input | What the answer shows | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Anchors A and B | `5413 PLAZA LLC` and `702 PLAZA LLC` | The same HPD business address (`746 61ST STREET`, 11220). One joint mortgage naming both as borrowers (`2021031601183001`). DOS filings on 2020-10-14 and 2020-10-15. Similar names. The DOS process address they share (5 Cromwell Circle, Staten Island) is the process address of five DOS entities. BOR placed them in no group (`no_linkage`) | The records do not say the two are owned together, and the answer does not either |

## Scenarios

- Similar names with shared records (for example an agent and addresses): reported as a possible connection with that evidence, not as the same entity.
- Similar names with no shared record: not reported as related.
- A similarly named entity's filing names a person or address missing from the first: reported as new evidence for that entity, with its source.
- A business address in a filing is searched: other companies, foundations or nonprofits that list it are returned with their filings. For a hub address the answer gives the count, says whether the list is complete or a sample, and supports no link.
- An organization's tax filing lists the address "care of" a management company: reported as an affiliation recorded in that filing, not as ownership.
