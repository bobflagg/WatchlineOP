# Q2: HPD-registered owner and registration dates

**Fixture status:** Examples are provisional until verified against the selected frozen snapshot, pinned BOR release, or versioned retrieval cache, as applicable. Existing local checks retain their stated query dates; they are not a frozen acceptance baseline. See [expected-answer policy](../roadmap.md#expected-answer-policy).

## Question

> Who is the HPD-registered owner of this building, and when was the registration last filed?

## Who asks and why

The registration names the owner of record for repairs and notices, and organizers address letters to it.

## Required content of an answer

1. The building (BIN) and its HPD **registration** (registration id).
2. The **registered owner** as recorded, with the recorded contact type.
3. The **date the registration was last filed** and the **registration end date**.

## Caveats the answer must carry

- An HPD registrant is who registered, not proof of title.
- State the registration period.
- The registered owner is not interchangeable with the deed grantee, a process contact or the managing agent.

## Cases seen in the local snapshot

- **Registration ended before the snapshot.** 234 54 Street (BBL `3008210019`): last registered 2020-08-31, period ended 2021-09-01. The answer reports the registered owner with those dates and does not present the registration as current.
- **No registration found.** 259 22 Street (BBL `3008970014`) has no HPD registration. The answer says none was found in the snapshot and infers no owner.
- **More than one registration for a building.** 1,269 BINs carry more than one registration. All are shown, and none is silently chosen.
- **No owner contact recorded.** 13,170 of 203,236 registrations have no `CorporateOwner`, `IndividualOwner` or `JointOwner` contact.
- **No last-registration date recorded.** 3,237 registrations have a null last-registration date.

Observations that matter for the model:

- The registration is **per building**. A lot with three buildings has three registrations.
- The same owner entity can be spelled differently across registrations of one lot.
- Registration end dates range from 1994-05-31 to 2027-09-01. 51,713 of 203,236 registrations had ended before the snapshot date.
- Owner contact types in the data are `CorporateOwner` (121,589), `IndividualOwner` (47,866) and `JointOwner` (44,594). Other types are `Agent`, `HeadOfficer`, `Officer`, `Shareholder`, `SiteManager` and `Lessee`. Which types count as the registered owner is not settled here.
- The table carries a last registration date and an end date, but no period start date.
