# Q2: HPD-registered owner and registration dates

**Fixture status:** Examples are provisional until verified against the selected frozen snapshot, pinned BOR release, or versioned retrieval cache, as applicable. Existing local checks retain their stated query dates; they are not a frozen acceptance baseline. See [expected-answer policy](../roadmap.md#expected-answer-policy).

## Question

> Who is the HPD-registered owner of this building, and when was the registration last filed?

### Sub-questions

Cleanup applied to the parent: the compound "who is the owner, and when was it last filed" is split into one question about the registrations and their dates and one about the owner on each. Identifiers, column names and source vocabulary are removed from the sub-question text; the hidden assumptions are listed below. Tests and ontology fragments reference these IDs. Q2 keeps its ID and passes when both pass.

| ID | Sub-question | Answer requirements covered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q2.1 | Which registrations are on record for this building, and for each, when was it last filed and when does its registration period end? | 1 (building, registration), 3 (dates) |
| Q2.2 | For each of those registrations, who is recorded as its owner, and in what capacity? | 2 (registered owner, contact type) |

Why two and not three: the dates are attributes of the same building and registration pair and come from one lookup, as the four characteristics of Q1.4 do. Splitting them out would add a test with the same fixtures and no separate graph element.

### Hidden assumptions made explicit

| Assumption in the original wording | Rule (where settled) |
| --- | --- |
| "the owner" implies one owner | A registration can have several owner contacts, or none. All are listed, none chosen, and none inferred when none is recorded (Q2.2) |
| "registered owner" means any contact on the registration | Only the three owner contact types count; agents, officers, site managers and others are different roles (decision 2026-11-12) |
| "the registration" implies one registration per building | A building can have several (1,059 do). All are shown (Q2.1) |
| "when was it last filed" has one answer per registration | Dates belong to the building and registration pair; rows can disagree, and then the date is absent (Q2.1) |
| "last filed" and "registered" imply the registration is current | The source gives an end date but no period start. Whether a registration is current is the agent's reading against a stated date, not stored (Q2.1) |
| "building" is the entry point | The building is a BIN known from Q1. A lot with no HPD registration has no building in the graph, so Q2 does not start there (Q1.2) |
| The owner is the owner of the building | The registration records who registered, not title (caveat carried by the vocabulary) |
| The name identifies one entity | Names are reported as recorded; spelling differences are neither merged nor read as different owners (Q2.2) |

## Who asks and why

The registration names the owner of record for repairs and notices, and organizers address letters to it.

## Required content of an answer

1. The building (BIN) and its HPD **registration** (registration id).
2. The **registered owner** as recorded, with the recorded contact type.
3. The **date the registration was last filed** and the **registration end date**.

## Answer contract

| Field | Concept | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bin` | BIN | The building asked about |
| `registrations` | HPD registration | One entry per registration. All are listed; none is chosen. Empty means "no HPD registration recorded for this building", not "no owner" |
| `registrations[].registrationId` | registration id | As recorded |
| `registrations[].lastRegistered` | last registered date | Absent when the source has no date or its rows disagree |
| `registrations[].registrationEnd` | registration end date | Absent when the source rows disagree. The agent states whether it is before or after the snapshot date (2026-05-29); the source has no period start |
| `registrations[].owners` | registered owner contact | `{contactType, name}` for every owner contact of the registration, names exactly as recorded. Empty means "no owner contact recorded" and no owner is inferred. A blank name is absent |

## Caveats the answer must carry

- An HPD registrant is who registered, not proof of title.
- State the registration period.
- The registered owner is not interchangeable with the deed grantee, a process contact or the managing agent.

## Cases seen in the local snapshot

- **Registration ended before the snapshot.** 234 54 Street (BBL `3008210019`): last registered 2020-08-31, period ended 2021-09-01. The answer reports the registered owner with those dates and does not present the registration as current.
- **No registration found.** 259 22 Street (BBL `3008970014`) has no HPD registration. The answer says none was found in the snapshot and infers no owner.
- **More than one registration for a building.** 1,059 valid BINs carry more than one registration (queried 2026-11-12; the earlier figure of 1,269 was not reproduced: 1,067 BINs counting every BIN value). All are shown, and none is silently chosen.
- **Placeholder registration id.** `registrationid` 0 sits on 3 rows for 3 unrelated buildings in 3 boroughs, with 27 contacts (6 owner type) mixed across them. It is excluded and counted; those buildings show no registration, and the answer says none is recorded in the usable data.
- **Dates differ between rows of one registration.** 23 registration ids carry different dates on different rows, so dates belong to the building and registration pair. For 9 pairs the rows disagree (or only some are null); the date is then absent, never picked.
- **Several owner contacts on one registration.** Joint owners, or an LLC and an LP (for example registration `121720`). All are listed.
- **Blank owner name.** 422 owner contacts (183 corporate, 239 individual or joint) have no name. They are kept with the name absent.
- **Duplicate contacts.** Exact duplicates (same registration, type and name) are one contact; `registrationcontactid` is not unique in the snapshot and is not used.
- **No owner contact recorded.** 13,170 of 203,236 registrations have no `CorporateOwner`, `IndividualOwner` or `JointOwner` contact.
- **No last-registration date recorded.** 3,237 registrations have a null last-registration date.

Observations that matter for the model:

- The registration is **per building**. A lot with three buildings has three registrations.
- The same owner entity can be spelled differently across registrations of one lot.
- Registration end dates range from 1994-05-31 to 2027-09-01. 51,713 of 203,236 registrations had ended before the snapshot date.
- Owner contact types in the data are `CorporateOwner` (121,589), `IndividualOwner` (47,866) and `JointOwner` (44,594). Other types are `Agent`, `HeadOfficer`, `Officer`, `Shareholder`, `SiteManager` and `Lessee`. **Decision 2026-11-12:** the three owner types are the registered owner; the others are not (see `specs/decisions.md`).
- The table carries a last registration date and an end date, but no period start date.
