## Question

> What mortgages, assignments and satisfactions are recorded against the lot, in what amounts and with which lenders?

## Who asks and why

The lender is the second party with leverage over repairs.

### Sub-questions

CLaRO-style cleanup (stage 1, 2026-11-17). The parent asks for the financing documents of a lot, their parties, and a separation by kind. Decision 2026-11-17 (user choice): all seven financing types are loaded for every lot in the graph, with every party row. Tests and ontology fragments reference these IDs. Q13 keeps its ID and passes when all three pass.

## Required content of an answer

1. Each financing document recorded against the lot: document type (with its description), document date, recorded date, amount, and parties (borrower, lender).
2. Satisfactions, assignments and terminations listed as separate documents.
3. The snapshot date.

## Caveats the answer must carry

- No satisfaction recorded is not proof the loan is still outstanding.
- A termination is not proof the loan was paid.
- The answer reports the documents and does not say which loan is outstanding.
- A conclusion drawn from a termination is derived (Type II), not recorded.
