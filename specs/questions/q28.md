## Question

> What Housing Court actions has HPD brought against this building's owner, by case type and status, and was any harassment finding made? Who is named as respondent, and does the respondent match the registered owner?

### Hidden assumptions made explicit (profiled 2026-11-20)

| Assumption | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| "This building's owner" | Cases are recorded against a building (BIN), not an owner. They hang on the building; the owner is the respondent's role as recorded |
| Scope | Cases on a registered building: 210,169 of 239,434 load. 211,652 are on registered lots, so 1,483 are excluded and counted (no usable BIN, or a BIN that is not a registered building) |
| "Harassment finding" | Only After Inquest (252 cases) and After Trial (78) are findings (217 in scope). 'No Harassment' (5,990 in scope) is a recorded outcome, not a finding; null means none recorded. One finding has no finding date: absent |
| Dates | 289 cases have no open date (absent). 3 are before 1990 (e.g. 1930) and 1 is after the snapshot (2030): kept as recorded, counted, reported as source errors, never corrected |
| Status | Free text as HPD records it (CLOSED, PENDING, and rare 'GRANTED - date' forms): kept as recorded, not normalised |
| Penalty | Whole dollars, recorded on 217 cases; null is absent, 0 is a recorded zero |
| Respondent | One comma-joined string (up to 13 parts, blank parts, 'ET AL'). Kept as recorded, with N3 keys of its parts; 2,919 loaded cases have none. Roles are not recorded |
| Match to owner | Registered owner = the three HPD owner contact types over every registration of the BIN. A part equal to an owner name after rule N3 is a lead. No respondent or no owner name is "not comparable". Anchor C's respondent (STREET) differs from HPD's owner (ST): not matched, not reconciled |
| Deed grantee | Not needed (the owner comparison is Q3's); `acris_parties` is not used |

## Who asks and why

Litigation shows enforcement beyond violations, and who the city named.

## Required content of an answer

1. Each case: type, open date, status, open judgement, harassment finding and its date, and penalty.
2. The respondent as recorded.
3. Whether the respondent matches the registered owner, and the basis.
4. The snapshot date.

## Caveats the answer must carry

- A case is an action brought, not a finding of fault, unless a finding is recorded.
- A respondent can be an owner, an agent or an individual. Roles are as recorded.
- A respondent matched only by name is a lead.
