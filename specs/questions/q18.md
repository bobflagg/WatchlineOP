## Question

> Which deed transfers among a portfolio's lots look like restructuring (a `$0` re-deed into single-purpose LLCs) rather than a sale, what supports that reading independently of the grouping itself, and what remains uncertain?

## Who asks and why

Repeated `$0` deeds can show a landlord moving buildings into new LLCs.

## Required content of an answer

1. Each deed: document id, document date, recorded date, amount, grantor, grantee.
2. A classification of each (sale, `$0` transfer, other) and what supports a restructuring reading, using evidence independent of the grouping being explained.
3. What remains uncertain.

## Caveats the answer must carry

- `$0` is a hint, not a finding.
- A buyer with no established relationship is "no relationship established".
- The grouping must not be used as its own evidence.
