# Q23: Managing agent footprint

Existing local checks retain their stated query dates; they are not a frozen acceptance baseline. See [expected-answer policy](../roadmap.md#expected-answer-policy).

## Question

> Which buildings does this managing agent disclose in HPD registrations, and how does that set relate to the building's Operational Network and Owner Portfolio? Is the agent an operator tied to the owner, or a commercial service?

## Who asks and why

A shared agent can connect buildings, or only show that one company manages many clients.

## Required content of an answer

1. The agent as recorded, with the spelling variants combined and the rule used.
2. The registrations and buildings that disclose it, and the contact type.
3. How many of those buildings are in the building's Operational Network and Owner Portfolio, and how many are not.
4. Whether the agent's address is on the mail-drop list.

## Caveats the answer must carry

- A shared agent is a management relationship, not ownership.
- A large footprint suggests a commercial service. It does not prove one.
- Spelling variants are combined by a stated rule, so the counts are derived.

## Worked examples

| Case | Input | What the answer shows | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Large footprint | `BRIGHTON MANAGEMENT` | 42 registrations disclose a name beginning `Brighton Management`, in three spellings: `BRIGHTON MANAGEMENT` (35), `Brighton Management` (5), `BRIGHTON MANAGEMENT LLC` (2). All carry contact type `GEN.PART` | Spelling variants change the count |
