## Question

> What complaints has HPD recorded for this building, by type, category and status, over what period, and what is the rate per residential unit?

### Sub-questions

CLaRO-style cleanup (stage 1, 2026-11-19). Decision (user choice): problems received since 2021-01-01 on HPD-registered lots are loaded as `Problem` nodes linked to the building (BIN). Q27 passes when both pass.

| ID | Sub-question | Answer requirements covered |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Q27.2 | What is the rate of problems per residential unit of its lot over that period? | 4 (rate), caveat on zero or missing units |
| Q27.3 | Which pest complaint problems does this building have in a period, by pest (mice, roaches, bed bugs and so on) and status, and how many are still open? | 1 (period), 2 (counts), 3 (open); extension of 2026-11-21 |

### Hidden assumptions made explicit (profiled 2026-11-19)

| Assumption | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| "Registered buildings" | A problem is loaded when its BBL is in HPD registrations and its BIN is a graph Building. 4,061,388 problems since 2021-01-01 are on registered lots; 4,033,827 load; 27,561 are excluded and counted |
| Period | Loaded window starts 2021-01-01 (15.0M rows on registered lots for all years, user chose the recent window). A period starting earlier is answered for 2021 onward and says so; never "none" |
| Counting unit | Problem records. HPD's duplicate flag (682,192 loaded) is kept as recorded; duplicates are counted in totals and reported apart. The anchor's 27 includes 1 flagged duplicate. Distinct complaints are returned too |
| Type | As HPD records: EMERGENCY, NON EMERGENCY, IMMEDIATE EMERGENCY, plus HAZARDOUS and REFERRAL, reported when present |
| Status | The problem's own status (OPEN or CLOSE) at the snapshot; the complaint's status differs on 7,423 loaded rows and is not stored |
| Category | Major category, and since the 2026-11-21 extension the minor category and the problem code (as HPD records them, text). Pest complaints are minor category `PESTS` (under major category `UNSANITARY CONDITION` only); the pest is the problem code: MICE, ROACHES, OTHER, BED BUGS, FLIES, TERMITES, FLEAS. Profiled on the 4,033,827 loaded rows: no null or blank value, 59 distinct minor categories, 349,021 `PESTS` problems (3,874 open, 714 flagged duplicate): MICE 147,185, ROACHES 130,774, OTHER 41,677, BED BUGS 15,259, FLIES 10,853, TERMITES 1,637, FLEAS 1,636. A minor category can sit under two major categories (CABINET, RADIATOR), so it is read with its major category; `OTHER` is not a pest name and never means mice. Only MICE says mice |
| Rate | Problems in the period divided by the lot's PLUTO residential units, over the whole period, not annualized. 0 or missing units gives "rate unavailable" |
| Apartment, anonymity, coordinates | Not stored |

## Who asks and why

Complaints show what tenants reported, including problems that were never cited.

## Required content of an answer

1. The period, and the snapshot date.
2. Counts by type (emergency, immediate emergency, non-emergency), major category and status.
3. How many are still open.
4. The rate per residential unit, with the unit count and its source.
5. For Q27.3: pest complaint problems by pest and status for the period, how many are still open, and the same caveats.

## Caveats the answer must carry

- The records are of problems, not of distinct complaints. One complaint can have several problems.
- They say what was reported, not what was found.
- Apartment, anonymity and exact coordinates are not used.
- A pest complaint is a tenant's report of mice, roaches or another pest, not a finding that the pest is present. The count is per building, not per unit; the agent cannot say how many units are affected. A pest violation (Q15.3) is what an inspector cited and is counted apart.
