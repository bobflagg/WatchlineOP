# Q10: Where the two layers disagree

## Question

> Where do the two layers disagree: an Operational Network containing several Owner Portfolios, or an Owner Portfolio spanning several Operational Networks? What evidence explains each difference?

## Who asks and why

It explains why two honest lists of "the landlord's buildings" differ.

## Required content of an answer

1. The Operational Network and the Owner Portfolio of the building, each with run, id and member counts in lots.
2. Members in one layer and not the other.
3. For each difference, the evidence that explains it (for example a business address linking lots in the network but not the portfolio, or a masked address that splits a portfolio across network fragments).

## Caveats the answer must carry

- Both layers are derived. Neither is the true list.
- Name the run and the counting unit.
