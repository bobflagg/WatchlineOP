# Q17: Missing, lapsed or stale registration

**Fixture status:** Examples are provisional until verified against the selected frozen snapshot, pinned BOR release, or versioned retrieval cache, as applicable. Existing local checks retain their stated query dates; they are not a frozen acceptance baseline. See [expected-answer policy](../roadmap.md#expected-answer-policy).

## Question

> Is this building's registration missing, lapsed, or older than the latest deed transfer? Does the building meet the unit threshold of the registration dataset (3+ units), so that a gap is worth reporting?

## Who asks and why

An unregistered or lapsed owner has no contact on file for tenants.

## Required content of an answer

1. Yes or no for each condition: missing, lapsed, older than the latest deed. Each with its dates.
2. The residential unit count and the dataset's 3+ unit threshold.
3. The snapshot date of each source.

## Caveats the answer must carry

- Do not rule on legal applicability beyond the stated threshold.
- Absence from the dataset is not a ruling that no registration exists. Newly built buildings can be unregistered for a legitimate reason, and the answer says it does not know.
