## Question

> Which buildings belong to this building's Operational Network in the selected run, which registration and identity links connect them, and was the network a whole component or a Louvain community?

## Definition

An **Operational Network** groups buildings that connect through registration infrastructure: shared names, business addresses or resolved identities, with aggregator addresses masked. It is built from connected components, and components over 300 lots are cut into communities by the Louvain method. It is a derived grouping, not a finding of common ownership.

## Who asks and why

It shows how many buildings share the same operation.

Note (2026-11-16): the Q8.3 answer also lists each landlord record's name and business addresses as WoW filed them, the HPD contacts each record is based on (`BASED_ON_CONTACT`, decision 2026-11-15: the record WoW used, not a finding of ownership), and every link with its weight and evidence. Landlord records cover lots through `COVERS`; they are labels on records, never verified owners. See `profiles/q08.yaml` and `tests/q8_3`.

## Required content of an answer

1. The source-data snapshot, pipeline run and release, and the run-scoped network id.
2. Members in lots (BBL), buildings (BIN) and residential units, with the counting unit named.
3. The registration and identity links that connect the members.
4. Whether the network is a whole connected component or a Louvain community.
5. If the building is in no network: the reason, from the list in the caveats.

## Caveats the answer must carry

- Name the run. Group ids are stable within a build, not across builds. A historical question needs historical evidence, not today's group id.
- A network is a lead to verify, not legal ownership.
- Absence of a link is not evidence of different owners. State which applies: no qualifying linkage; excluded by policy (co-op or condo, a masked aggregator address); below the eligibility rule; outside source coverage; outside method input.
- For a subdivided network, separate connectivity in the original component from membership in the selected community. Two buildings can have a path before subdivision and still fall in different networks.
