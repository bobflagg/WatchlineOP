# Q5: NY DOS record for the owner entity

## Question

> What does the NY DOS active-entity record show for this owner entity: type, filing date, DOS process name and address, and registered agent? Where the record is absent or silent, what is known and from what source?

### Sub-questions

CLaRO-style cleanup (stage 1). The parent asks for a record, three attribute groups and a fallback. Name comparison is computed at answer time (rules in `src/watchline/compare.py`) and not stored. Q5 passes when all parts pass.

| Q5.2 | To which name and address does the state forward legal papers for that record? | 2 (process name and address) |

## Who asks and why

It shows whether the owner is a single-purpose shell and where legal papers go.

## Required content of an answer

1. The matched DOS record: DOS id, entity name, entity type, jurisdiction, initial filing date.
2. The DOS process name and address, and the registered agent if listed.
3. The name comparison used to match (exact, or the normalization rule), and the snapshot date.
4. If no record: "no record in the active-entity snapshot", with the spellings tried.

## Caveats the answer must carry

- "No record in the active-entity snapshot" is not "does not exist". Dissolved entities are absent.
- The DOS process name and address are where the state forwards legal service. They do not say who runs the entity.
- No officers, managers or members are carried for LLCs. The CEO and location fields are empty for LLCs: report "not carried for this entity type".
- Commercial registered agents and the statutory default process name are not people and not leads.

## Scenarios

- The DOS record names only a process address and registered agent: retrieve the entity's state filing and attachments, and report any manager, member or officer the documents name, each with document, page and extraction method.
- The only agents named are corporate service companies: reported as such, with no person or address treated as a lead.
- A name read from a scanned document is uncertain: reported with the extraction method and a low-confidence flag, not used as a link on its own.
- A retrieved filing gives a different address from the dataset: both are returned with their dates and the answer does not choose.
- The entity is not in the dataset: "no record in the active-entity snapshot". Then look for a retrieved filing or an ACRIS or HPD mention, each labeled by source.
