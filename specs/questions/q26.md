# Q26: What each absence means

**Fixture status:** Examples are provisional until verified against the selected frozen snapshot, pinned BOR release, or versioned retrieval cache, as applicable. Existing local checks retain their stated query dates; they are not a frozen acceptance baseline. See [expected-answer policy](../roadmap.md#expected-answer-policy).

## Question

> For this address, which sources hold no record (HPD registration, DOS entity, either layer, any violation), and what does each absence mean?

## Who asks and why

"Nothing found" means different things, and an organizer should not read it as "nothing there".

## Required content of an answer

For each source with no record: the source, its snapshot date, and one reason:
- no qualifying linkage
- excluded by policy
- below the eligibility rule
- outside source coverage
- outside method input

The answer also says recall is unmeasured where it applies.

## Caveats the answer must carry

- Absence from a snapshot is not a finding about the world.
- No violation record means none in the snapshot, not that conditions are good.
