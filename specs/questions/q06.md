# Q6: Aggregator or mail-drop address

**Fixture status:** Examples are provisional until verified against the selected frozen snapshot, pinned BOR release, or versioned retrieval cache, as applicable. Existing local checks retain their stated query dates; they are not a frozen acceptance baseline. See [expected-answer policy](../roadmap.md#expected-answer-policy).

## Question

> Was this address masked or down-weighted by a given method as an aggregator address, why, and is it also on the curated mail-drop list?

## Who asks and why

It tells an organizer that a shared address is a service address, so it is not a lead.

## Required content of an answer

1. The address and how many HPD parties and buildings carry it.
2. Per method (Operational Network, Owner Portfolio): masked or down-weighted, yes or no, with the reason, the method version and the run.
3. Whether the address is on the curated mail-drop and registered-agent list, and the classification given there.

## Caveats the answer must carry

- Masking is a method's decision. Name the method and the run.
- Method masking and the curated list are separate classifications and are reported separately.
- Co-location at a masked or mail-drop address supports no ownership or identity claim.
