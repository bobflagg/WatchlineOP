# Q16: Reports and filing documents

**Fixture status:** Examples are provisional until verified against the selected frozen snapshot, pinned BOR release, or versioned retrieval cache, as applicable. Existing local checks retain their stated query dates; they are not a frozen acceptance baseline. See [expected-answer policy](../roadmap.md#expected-answer-policy).

## Question

> What do published reports and retrieved filing documents say about this owner or entity, how do they relate to the recorded facts, and what is each one's review status?

### Sub-questions

CLaRO-style cleanup (stage 1, 2026-11-18). Q16 is retrieval-extraction only: no source is in the frozen snapshot, so there is no graph element, loader, `expected.sql` or Cypher lookup. The entry is an entity name or an address; a name is compared by rule N3 and a name match is a lead. Items are session evidence and are never loaded into the graph. Each item is an answer-contract record (the fields under "Required content of an answer"); the cache and fixtures are supplied for the downstream consumer.

| ID | Sub-question | Answer requirements covered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q16.1 | Which published reports name this entity or address, from which publisher and date, and what claim does each state (quoted)? | 1, 2, 3 (`published_reporting`) |
| Q16.2 | Which retrieved filing documents name this entity or address, from which body and page, and what value was extracted, by what method and with what confidence? | 1, 2, 3 (`public_record`, `web_content`) |
| Q16.3 | How does each item relate to the recorded facts (agrees, adds, conflicts, unrelated), and on what basis is it treated as about this entity (exact name, exact address, or a lead only)? | 4, 5 |
| Q16.4 | What is each item's review status, and which are contested, dated after the as-of date, unreadable or behind payment; when nothing is found, which queries and dates were searched? | 3, acceptable incomplete |

Decisions and findings (2026-11-18):

| Finding | Rule it produced |
| --- | --- |
| No Q16 source is in the snapshot or the graph | No loader, SQL or Cypher. Retrieval-extraction acceptance only, on a versioned cache the user supplies (like Q5.4 and Q11.5); stays `pending` until the downstream consumer runs it |
| A report is a claim that was published | The publication is the recorded evidence; truth and applicability are separate. `review_status` starts `unreviewed`; the project never sets `confirmed` or `refuted` itself |
| Similar but not identical names, and a name on a scan read with low confidence | Basis `lead only` (N3 inequality or low-confidence flag); never used as a link or to join recorded facts |
| A person named in a claim or a signatory | Shown with the source and the role the item gives; the recorded role in the filings is returned beside it; no ownership is inferred |
| Two contradicting items | Both returned, status `contested`, neither chosen |
| An item dated after the as-of date, or superseded by later records | Reported with both dates; an item after the as-of date is outside the answer and listed as such |
| Nothing found | "no reports or documents found in the sources searched" with queries and dates; never "no news" |
| Relation to the recorded facts is a comparison against HPD, ACRIS and DOS values | Computed at answer time from the cited recorded item; not stored |

## Who asks and why

News and filings answer "has there been notable news about my landlord".

## Required content of an answer

Inputs: entity or address; sources searched; as-of date; step and time budget.

For each item:
1. Publisher or filing body, date, URL or document id, and page.
2. The quoted claim or extracted value, and the extraction method.
3. `source_category` (`public_record`, `published_reporting`, `web_content`, `project_derived`) and `review_status` (`unreviewed`, `confirmed`, `refuted`, `inconclusive`, `contested`).
4. How it relates to the recorded facts: agrees, adds, conflicts, or unrelated.
5. The basis for treating it as about this entity (exact name or address, or a lead only).

## Acceptable incomplete

- Nothing found: "no reports or documents found in the sources searched", with the queries and dates. Never "no news" or "no issues".
- A document unreadable, partial or behind payment: an unresolved lead with the reason.
- An uncertain scanned name: reported with a low-confidence flag and not used as a link.

## Failures to avoid

- A claim stated as fact.
- Ownership inferred from a report or from a document's signatory.
- A person's name shown without its source and role.
- An entity matched on name alone without saying so.
- Contradicting reports resolved silently.
- A missing report read as nothing having happened.

## Test cases

- Ordinary: an article naming the exact LLC; a retrieved filing with a named manager.
- Difficult: an article naming a similarly named entity; two contradicting articles; a scanned attachment with an uncertain name; a paid-only registry step; a report dated after the as-of date.

## Caveats the answer must carry

A report is a claim that was published, not a fact. The record that it was published is recorded evidence. Whether the claim is true and applies is a separate, derived judgment. Web and document findings are session evidence, not graph facts.

## Scenarios

- A news article states that a fund controls the owner's parent company: shown as a reported claim, with publisher, date and quoted text, unreviewed, and kept apart from the Owner Portfolio.
- Two articles contradict each other: both shown, contested, neither chosen.
- A claim names a person whose recorded role in the filings differs: both returned, and the person shown only in the recorded role.
- A claim from before the as-of date is superseded by later records: reported with both dates.
- An article names an LLC with a similar but not identical name: reported as a lead about a different entity.
- A retrieved filing is partly unreadable: reported with the extraction method and a low-confidence flag.
