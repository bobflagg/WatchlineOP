## Question

> What evidence supports an ownership, parent-company, management, financing or other affiliation involving this entity, which relationship is documented, and which is only hypothesized?

## Who asks and why

It answers "who are the people and companies behind my landlord".

## Required content of an answer

1. Each relationship with its **type** (ownership, parent, management, financing, other) and the evidence behind it.
2. Each marked **documented** (recorded) or **hypothesized** (derived lead).
3. Source record, date and snapshot for each.

## Caveats the answer must carry

- Lender, agent and shared-borrower records are different relationship types.
- Only a documented parent relationship may be called one.
- A lender is a financing relationship, not control or parenthood.
- A disclosed managing agent is a management relationship, not ownership.
- A reported claim of a parent relationship is shown as reported, not as documented.
