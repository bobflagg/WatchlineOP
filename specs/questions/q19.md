# Q19: Membership explanation

**Fixture status:** Examples are provisional until verified against the selected frozen snapshot, pinned BOR release, or versioned retrieval cache, as applicable. Existing local checks retain their stated query dates; they are not a frozen acceptance baseline. See [expected-answer policy](../roadmap.md#expected-answer-policy).

## Question

> Why are these two buildings grouped together, or left unlinked, in each layer? Give the supporting records, linkage stages, connecting path, exclusions and the run.

## Who asks and why

It lets an organizer check a claim before repeating it.

## Required content of an answer

Inputs: two buildings (BBL); layer or layers; run.

Per layer: same group, different groups, or one or both ungrouped (with reason code).

For the same group: composition, a representative edge path with the stage and records behind each edge, and full detail on request. For networks: whether the pair was connected in the original component, and whether it was subdivided. The benchmark precision of the stages used, as a method property.

## Acceptable incomplete

Edges not exported: membership only, with the statement that evidence is not available.

## Failures to avoid

- Different owners inferred from a non-link.
- Direct evidence claimed where there is only a path.
- Stage precision presented as a link probability.
- Pre-subdivision connectivity presented as network membership.

## Test cases

- Ordinary: a direct identity link.
- Difficult: a path through a third lot; a masked hub; a Louvain-split pair connected before subdivision.

## Caveats the answer must carry

- Name the run and the layer.
- Reason codes for ungrouped buildings: no qualifying linkage; excluded by policy; below the eligibility rule; outside source coverage; outside method input.
- A method's benchmark precision is not the probability that a particular link is correct.
- Absence of a link is not evidence of different owners.
