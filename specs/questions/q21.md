# Q21: Violations across a layer

**Fixture status:** Examples are provisional until verified against the selected frozen snapshot, pinned BOR release, or versioned retrieval cache, as applicable. Existing local checks retain their stated query dates; they are not a frozen acceptance baseline. See [expected-answer policy](../roadmap.md#expected-answer-policy).

## Question

> For a chosen layer (Owner Portfolio or Operational Network), which of its buildings have the most open hazardous violations per unit, and how concentrated are they? How does the answer change between the two layers?

## Who asks and why

It shows which of a landlord's buildings are the worst, for a coordinated campaign.

## Required content of an answer

1. The layer, run and member lots.
2. Open violations per lot, reported **by class** (A, B, C) and not combined under the word "hazardous".
3. A ranked list, with the rate per residential unit and the unit count and its source.
4. A concentration figure (for example the share held by the top lots).
5. The same measure for the other layer, and how the list changes.

## Caveats the answer must carry

- A violation is what an inspector cited, not a measure of conditions.
- The list changes with the layer used. Both layers are derived.
- A lot's residential units are lot-wide and are not summed across its buildings.
- Violations are as of the snapshot date.
