# Q21: Violations across a layer

**Fixture status:** Examples are provisional until verified against the selected frozen snapshot, pinned BOR release, or versioned retrieval cache, as applicable. Existing local checks retain their stated query dates; they are not a frozen acceptance baseline. See [expected-answer policy](../roadmap.md#expected-answer-policy).

## Question

> For a chosen layer (Owner Portfolio or Operational Network), which of its buildings have the most open hazardous violations per unit, and how concentrated are they? How does the answer change between the two layers?

### Sub-questions

CLaRO-style cleanup (stage 1, 2026-11-19). No new graph element: members (`IN_NETWORK`, `IN_PORTFOLIO`), buildings (`HAS_BUILDING`), violations (`HAS_VIOLATION`) and PLUTO units exist (Q8, Q9, Q15). Per-lot counts, rates, ranks and the concentration figure are computed at answer time. The parent passes when all three pass.

| ID | Sub-question | Answer requirements covered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q21.1 | For a group in a layer, how many open violations does each member lot have, by class, and what is its rate per residential unit, ranked? | 1 (layer, run, lots), 2 (by class), 3 (ranked, rate, unit source) |
| Q21.2 | What share of the group's open violations do its top lots hold? | 4 (concentration) |
| Q21.3 | For one lot, how do the same measures compare between its Operational Network and its Owner Portfolio, and which lots drop out of or join the list? | 5 (other layer, change) |

### Hidden assumptions made explicit

| Assumption | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| "Hazardous" | Not a class. Classes A, B and C (and I where present) are reported apart and never added into a severity score |
| "Open" | Open at the snapshot (2026-07-02), as in Q15 |
| Violations of a lot | Distinct violations of the lot's buildings (violations hang on the BIN). A BIN on several lots counts toward each lot; a group's distinct total is also returned so the overlap is visible |
| Rate | Lot open count over the lot's PLUTO residential units (lot-wide, never summed over buildings). Units 0 or absent: "rate unavailable", ranked last |
| Ranking | By rate (available first), then by open count; ties share no hidden order (bbl breaks them) |
| Concentration | Share of the sum of lot open counts held by the top 3 lots, with the count of lots that have any open violation. Stated with the member count |
| Members | Operational Network: all member lots. Owner Portfolio: rental member lots only (Q9). Group ids are valid only within the run |
| Absent layer | A lot in no group in a layer returns that layer as absent with its reason (Q19.1), not an empty list |

## Who asks and why

It shows which of a landlord's buildings are the worst, for a coordinated campaign.

## Required content of an answer

1. The layer, run and member lots.
2. Open violations per lot, reported **by class** (A, B, C) and not combined under the word "hazardous".
3. A ranked list, with the rate per residential unit and the unit count and its source.
4. A concentration figure (for example the share held by the top lots).
5. The same measure for the other layer, and how the list changes.

## Caveats the answer must carry

- A violation is what an inspector cited, not a measure of conditions.
- The list changes with the layer used. Both layers are derived.
- A lot's residential units are lot-wide and are not summed across its buildings.
- Violations are as of the snapshot date.
