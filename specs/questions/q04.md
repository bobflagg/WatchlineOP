## Question

> Who is the managing agent, head officer or site manager HPD lists for this building?

## Who asks and why

The agent and site manager are who a tenant can actually reach.

## Required content of an answer

1. The building's HPD registration and its registration dates.
2. Each listed contact with its **recorded role** (managing agent, head officer, site manager, and any other recorded role) and the recorded title.
3. The business address recorded for each contact.
4. The snapshot date.

## Caveats the answer must carry

- Show a person only in the HPD role recorded.
- A managing agent is a management relationship, not ownership.

A shared address is not evidence of identity |
