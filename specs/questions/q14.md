# Q14: Title holder and registered owner on a violation date

## Question

> Which title holders and HPD-registered owners do the records support for the date of a given violation, and what gaps or conflicts prevent a definite attribution?

### Sub-questions

CLaRO-style cleanup (stage 1, 2026-11-17). The parent asks for two attributions on one date and for their gaps. The date comes from a violation, which is **not loaded** (user choice, 2026-11-17: 1,970,575 rows); the agent gets its dates from a source tool, and the caller says which one counts. No new graph element: deeds (Q3, Q12), registrations and owner contacts (Q2) exist. Q14 passes when all three pass.

| ID | Sub-question | Answer requirements covered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q14.1 | Which deeds support the title holder of this lot on this date, under the chosen basis (document date or recorded date)? | 1 (title holders, document and recorded dates), 4 (gaps: no deed, not yet recorded, ties) |
| Q14.2 | Which HPD registrations of the lot's buildings are in force on this date, with their registration dates and owner contacts, and which are not (registered later, ended, undated)? | 2 (registered owners, periods), 4 (lapsed, filed later) |
| Q14.3 | Do the title holders and the registered owners coincide, differ, or cannot be compared? | 3 (coincide), 4 (conflicts) |

### Hidden assumptions made explicit (profiled 2026-11-17)

| Assumption | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| "The date of the violation" | Input. Inspection, notice-issued and other dates differ (141,635 of 1,970,575 violations have inspection on or after the notice date; 172,342 have no notice date; 4 have no inspection date; 2,403 have notice before inspection). The caller chooses; the answer names it |
| "Title holder on a date" | The deeds with the latest date on or before it. Basis `document`: the document date, else the recorded date (as now known; the deed may be unrecorded on the date). Basis `recorded`: the recorded date (what public record showed). Deeds that tie are all returned, none is chosen. A deed dated before and recorded after the date is flagged `recordedAfterDate`. CORRD and CONDEED are flagged confirmatory (anchor: 3008380044, deed dated 2023-01-31, recorded 2023-05-02, violation inspected 2023-03-17) |
| "Registered owner in force" | HPD keeps one row per registration with the last registration date and an end date, no start and no earlier filing: today's version of the past. Status: `registered-later` (last registered after the date), `ended`, `undated`, `end-unknown`, `in-force`. Anchor A has one registration last filed 2025-09-30, so nothing is in force on 2022-10-20 (a gap, not an absent owner). In force is HPD's period, not responsibility |
| Coincide | Computed: a grantee of a supporting deed equals (rule N3) a corporate owner of a registration in force. Individual owners are not comparable (spelled in a different order). A difference can be spelling; both names are shown labelled by source. A name lead, not a finding |
| Absence | No deed on or before the date is "no title record in the snapshot". A title holder missing from DOS says nothing about the entity on the date (DOS holds active entities only) |

## Who asks and why

It shows who was responsible when a violation was issued.

## Required content of an answer

Inputs: a violation, or a building and a date; which date counts (inspection, notice or issue); perspective ("as now known").

Output:
1. Title holders supported for that date, from deeds, with document and recorded dates.
2. Registered owners in force on that date, with registration periods.
3. Whether they coincide.
4. Each gap or conflict.

## Acceptable incomplete

- A title holder supported but no registration in force.
- "Cannot attribute", with the reason.
- The records are today's version of the past, and the answer says so.
- A title holder absent from the DOS data is "no record in the active-entity snapshot". It says nothing about whether the entity existed on the date, because the dataset holds active entities only.

## Failures to avoid

- The current owner substituted for the historical one.
- One owner asserted despite a conflict.
- The owner of record presented as the party responsible.

## Test cases

- Ordinary: one deed, continuous registration.
- Difficult: a deed dated before and recorded after the violation date; a lapsed registration; a sale between inspection and notice; a past title holder that has since dissolved and is missing from DOS.

## Caveats the answer must carry

Date semantics must be explicit: document date, recorded date, registration period, and violation dates are different. A role-level `valid_from` and `valid_to` is not enough.
