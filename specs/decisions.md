# Accepted decisions

A lightweight log of accepted project and modeling choices. Add the date, decision, and a short rationale when a new choice is accepted. Git retains the editing history; separate decision records and approval tooling are not required for the POC.

| Date | Decision | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-11-11 | Separate graph, retrieval/extraction, and investigation acceptance. WatchlineOP delivers the graph, mappings, and acceptance fixtures/contracts; the research agent is a downstream consumer. | A validated graph does not establish that document extraction or a complete investigation works. |
| 2026-11-11 | Use a frozen JustFix/WoW snapshot as the sole authority for database-backed test inputs and expected answers. Keep BOR releases and retrieval caches separately identified. | Tests should be reproducible and must not compare snapshot-backed answers against changing live records. |
| 2026-11-11 | Keep question IDs and concept IRIs stable; track semantic artifacts and tests in Git; use this decision log and one identified source snapshot. Rebuild the POC graph and rerun existing tests after accepted model changes. | Keep iteration reproducible with minimal overhead. Formal versioning, migrations, and release governance wait until the POC is viable. |
| 2026-11-11 | Use separate namespaces and identifiers for SKOS concepts, ontology classes/properties, and instances. Record concept-to-element traceability in a compact table; reuse SKOS IRIs directly only where explicitly modeled as controlled values. | Controlled meanings, formal modeling elements, and real-world instances have different roles. Explicit mappings preserve traceability without automatic class/property conversion or additional POC machinery. |
| 2026-11-11 | Prefer shallow, justified taxonomies without a fixed depth. Allow multiple parents and overlapping siblings; justify disjointness separately. Review label ambiguity and disconnected concepts in context rather than prohibit all shared labels or force artificial links. | Keep the vocabulary faithful to the questions and source meanings instead of shaping it to arbitrary structural rules or validator warnings. |
| 2026-11-11 | Make physical source mappings and Neo4j schema explicit, preserve direct lot-level Q1 scalar properties and exact integer cents, and require source-value reconciliation plus complete validation. Pin the exercised toolchain; use bounded checks and POC rebuilds. | Ontology meaning and shape compilation alone do not specify safe ingestion, physical encodings, identity integrity, or correct persisted answers. A small implementation contract provides these without a release framework. |
| 2026-11-11 | Use repository-relative links for internal documents and public URLs plus dependency notes for external references and producers. | The standalone specification must not depend on a personal filesystem layout or an undeclared sibling checkout. |
| 2026-11-11 | Freeze the Q1 evidence baseline on PLUTO release 26v1 (`pluto_latest`) and HPD registrations as of 2026-05-29 (`hpd_registrations`). Dump identifier and checksum: **pending, to be recorded in `data/README.md`.** | Q1 needs a fixed input and expected-answer authority before any graph test runs. Other sources (ACRIS, DOS, violations, etc.) are frozen when the questions that use them are started. |
| 2026-11-11 | BINs come only from HPD registrations. Q1 accepts that a lot with no HPD registration has no BIN in the graph. | PLUTO has no BIN column, and HPD registrations are the only BIN source in the frozen baseline. Completeness is limited to registered buildings, and the answer must say so. |
| 2026-11-11 | Address variant matching and resolution outcome (exact, variant, ambiguous, not found) are Geosupport's job, not the graph's. The graph stores the resolved BBL and the cached Geosupport response as evidence. | Geosupport is the authority for NYC address normalization. Reimplementing it in the graph would duplicate it and drift from it. |
| 2026-11-11 | Stage 1 setup. Namespace bases: concepts `https://watchline.example/vocab/`, ontology elements `https://watchline.example/onto/`, instances `https://watchline.example/id/` (placeholder domain). Concept IDs are readable slugs (e.g. `vocab:tax-lot`), stable once minted. Vocabulary file: `vocabulary/watchline.ttl`. | Small, hand-reviewed POC: readable IDs keep diffs and traces legible. Placeholder domain avoids implying live URLs. |
| 2026-11-11 | Q1 vocabulary: address resolution outcome labels are "matched exactly", "matched by variant", "ambiguous", "not found". "Parcel" is an alternative label of "tax lot", not a separate concept. | Outcome labels read clearly in a dossier and avoid clashing with "variant" as a street-name spelling. The specs treat parcel and tax lot as the same thing. |
| 2026-11-11 | Q1 vocabulary accepted (30 concepts; `validate_skos`: 0 violations, 0 warnings). `BBL` and `BIN` are separate identifier concepts, kept as top concepts because they fail the is-a test against tax lot and building. `addressed building` is a role under `building`. `unit count` (a reported tally) is distinct from `residential units` (a source-reported characteristic). `HPD registration` is minted with Q1 scope (source of BINs); Q2 extends it. | Keeps identifier, thing, role, characteristic and tally apart, as the evidence rules require. Defers parties on a registration to the question that needs them. |
| 2026-11-11 | Stage 2 setup. Layout: `profiles/evidence.yaml`, `profiles/<source_key>.yaml`, `profiles/qNN-answer.yaml`, `profiles/crosswalk-qNN.yaml`. Field names `snake_case`. Allowed values reference a SKOS scheme or an enum/range in the YAML. `make profiles` runs `scripts/check_profiles.py` (pyyaml, rdflib) and fails on any malformed file, unknown concept, incomplete answer field, uncrosswalked or undeclared column, spec-enum mismatch, or check that did not run. Assertion type values: `type_i`, `type_ii`, `reported_claim`. | One layout and one check for all questions; a skipped check must fail rather than look like a pass. |
| 2026-11-11 | `source_category` is spelled `project_derived` everywhere (CLAUDE.md, use-case.md, q16.md, data/sources.yaml). | snake_case matches the other category values and the YAML convention. |
| 2026-11-11 | Concept-level provenance: every concept has `dct:source` pointing to its question file section, source inventory or physical contract. Q1's 30 concepts done; `validate_skos`: 0 violations, 0 warnings. | Lets a reader trace a definition to the document that supports it. |
| 2026-11-11 | Q1 stage 2 profiles written (PLUTO and HPD registrations inspected 2026-11-11; Geosupport and 6 answer fields marked `pending` for lack of cached responses and unsettled name normalization). `numbldgs` added to `pluto` `columns_used`. | Exit criterion 2 for Q1 except the pending fields, which wait on recorded Geosupport responses. |
| 2026-11-11 | Stage 3 setup. Hierarchies stay in `vocabulary/watchline.ttl`; a code list gets its own `skos:ConceptScheme` only when it is a standalone value set. `vocabulary/taxonomy-notes.md` records each hierarchy's meaning, per-link justification (Links tables), exceptions, and Stage 5 disjointness candidates. `skos:Collection` is used for groupings that fail the is-a test. `make taxonomy` runs `scripts/check_taxonomy.py` (rdflib) and fails on a broader cycle, a missing or cross-scheme endpoint, a link without a justification row, a mixed-meaning branch, or multiple parents without an exception note; a check that did not run fails. | Settles the open one-file-or-many question for the POC. Keeps every hierarchy link justified and a skipped check from looking like a pass. |
| 2026-11-11 | Q1 taxonomy accepted. `addressed-building` loses `skos:broader building` (a role, fails is-a) and becomes a top concept with `skos:related building`; its ID is unchanged. Kept as is-a: `alternate-address` under `street-address`, the four characteristics under `source-reported-characteristic`, and `parcel-count`, `building-count` and `unit-count` under `reported-count`. `bbl` and `bin` stay top concepts. The four outcome values stay a flat scheme. Building class codes are not minted or nested. No collections were needed. `validate_skos`: 0 violations, 0 warnings (two info notes: no `dcterms:publisher` on the two schemes, deferred). No link is translated into an axiom, and no disjointness is declared. | Q1 needs no deep hierarchy. Only links that pass the is-a test remain; role, identifier and value-set concepts stay flat by intent. |
| 2026-11-11 | Stage 4 setup. Related links are written in both directions with plain `skos:related`; the association kind and justification live in `vocabulary/thesaurus-notes.md` (Related links table, Association kinds list). Labels stay plain SKOS; SKOS-XL is adopted only for a label that needs its own metadata. External mappings go in `vocabulary/mappings/external.sssom.tsv` (SSSOM), never in `watchline.ttl`. Target schemes: schema.org, PROV-O, W3C ORG (Wikidata not chosen). `make thesaurus` runs `scripts/check_thesaurus.py` (rdflib, pyyaml) and fails on a missing inverse, a related pair also connected by `skos:broader`, a pair without a notes row, kind or justification, a concept outside a declared scheme, a malformed SSSOM row, or a check that did not run. | One layout and one check for all questions; the kind of association stays out of the vocabulary file, as in ISO 25964. |
| 2026-11-11 | Q1 thesaurus accepted. 22 related pairs (44 triples), each with a kind and justification; the 7 Stage 1 one-way links now have inverses and 15 new pairs were added. Labels: `structure`, `address` and `apartments` moved to hidden labels (too generic or ambiguous to display, once Q2 adds business addresses); `parcel` and `lot` kept as alternative labels of `tax-lot`. Mappings: `street-address` `closeMatch` schema:PostalAddress; `source-snapshot` and `source-record` `broadMatch` prov:Entity; no match found for other concepts in schema.org, PROV-O or W3C ORG. `validate_skos`: 0 violations, 0 warnings (two info notes: no `dcterms:publisher`, deferred). `make thesaurus`, `make taxonomy`, `make profiles` pass. No concept ID changed. | Associations a Q1 reader needs to move between branches, none duplicating a hierarchy. Weakest true mapping predicate used. |
